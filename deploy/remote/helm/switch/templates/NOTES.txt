⚠  BACK UP YOUR DATA — this chart ships no backup jobs.
The Tuwunel volume ({{ include "switch.fullname" . }}-tuwunel-data) holds the
Matrix signing key: your homeserver's identity. Lose it and every account is
orphaned and this server can never be restored — so back it up. Also back up
Postgres (all app + Mattermost state). See BACKUP.md and
samples/volumesnapshot.example.yaml for the recommended approaches
(CSI VolumeSnapshots, pg_dump / managed-DB backups, Velero).
